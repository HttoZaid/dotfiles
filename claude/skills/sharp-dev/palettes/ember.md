# Ember Palette

**Mood:** Bold · Energetic · Consumer · Vibrant
**Best for:** Consumer apps, fitness, food delivery, gaming, entertainment, landing pages
**Avoid for:** Finance, healthcare, B2B enterprise

```

Ember 100   #FFD6C8    Badge bg
Ember 200   #FFB09A    Hover tint

Ember 400   #F55A38    Secondary accent
Ember 500   #E8360E    ★ PRIMARY ACCENT
Ember 600   #C22608    Hover state
Ember 700   #961A05    Text on light bg
Ember 800   #6A1003    Dark
Ember 900   #3E0801    Deepest
```
