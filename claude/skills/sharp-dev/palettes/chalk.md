# Chalk Palette

**Mood:** Pure white · Ultra-minimal · Clinical · Open
**Best for:** Medical apps, notes/writing tools, documentation, reading apps, clean portfolios
**Avoid for:** Gaming, entertainment, food apps (too sterile)

## Color Ramp (Accent: Graphite)

```
Graphite 50   #F8F8F8    Lightest bg tint
Graphite 100  #F0F0F0    Badge bg
Graphite 200  #E0E0E0    Hover tint
Graphite 300  #C8C8C8    Light accent
Graphite 400  #A0A0A0    Secondary accent
Graphite 500  #787878    ★ PRIMARY ACCENT
Graphite 600  #585858    Hover state
Graphite 700  #383838    Text on light bg
Graphite 800  #202020    Dark
Graphite 900  #101010    Deepest
```
