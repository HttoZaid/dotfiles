# Rose Palette

**Mood:** Soft · Approachable · Warm · Caring
**Best for:** Health & wellness apps, mental health tools, beauty, baby/parenting, self-care
**Avoid for:** B2B enterprise, finance, developer tools

## Color Ramp

```
Rose 50     #FEF0F4    Lightest pink tint
Rose 100    #FDD4E0    Badge bg
Rose 200    #FAB4C8    Hover tint
Rose 300    #F590AE    Light accent
Rose 400    #EC6C92    Secondary accent
Rose 500    #DC4E7A    ★ PRIMARY ACCENT
Rose 600    #B83860    Hover state
Rose 700    #8C2648    Text on light bg
Rose 800    #601630    Dark
Rose 900    #340818    Deepest
```
